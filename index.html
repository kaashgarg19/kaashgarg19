IoT Analytics for Predicting Human Movement near IoT Devices

My MSc dissertation explored the use of IoT data and machine learning techniques to predict human movement near IoT devices. The dissertation provides the foundation for a research extension that examines rare-event prediction, temporal evaluation and generalisation across sensing devices.
