01 About

I like starting with the data, then asking what the model is really telling us.

My background is in Computer Science, with postgraduate study in Advanced Computer Science. My strongest academic interest is in data-driven systems: understanding a dataset, testing assumptions, comparing evaluation choices and being clear about what the evidence can and cannot support.

My MSc dissertation introduced me to environmental IoT data and human-movement prediction. I have since separated the original work from a later research extension so the progression is transparent.

I also have software-development experience, including React Native. I keep that work in the portfolio as supporting technical evidence rather than making it the centre of my research profile.

02 Research

Featured research

Robust human-movement prediction from environmental IoT sensors

My original MSc project asked whether environmental sensor measurements could help predict human movement. The research extension asks a harder question: how reliable are those predictions when observations come from a different time period or sensing device?
